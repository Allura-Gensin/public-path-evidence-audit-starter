# Public-path review checklist

## Before reviewing

- [ ] Identify one visitor action and up to five public routes.
- [ ] Record the date, time, browser, and viewport.
- [ ] Confirm no login, data entry, submission, purchase, or private system
      access is needed.

## On each route

- [ ] Does the visible headline match the visitor action?
- [ ] Is the next action discoverable without guessing?
- [ ] Do CTA labels, destination, pricing or trial language, and trust pages
      agree with one another?
- [ ] Is the same public route usable at desktop and mobile widths?
- [ ] Is each observation reproducible and limited to what was visible?

## Before sharing findings

- [ ] Separate observation, question, and recommendation.
- [ ] Record a clean route as a pass when that is what was observed.
- [ ] Avoid claims about revenue, security, accessibility, legal compliance, or
      performance unless separately established.
