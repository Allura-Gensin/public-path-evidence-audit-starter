# $350 fixed-scope public-path audit

This is the standard scope for an Allure Labs Launch-Path Evidence Audit. It
helps a buyer decide whether to request a written scope conversation; it is not
a contract, testing authorization, invoice, or payment request.

## Included

- One agreed public visitor path across up to five public routes.
- Desktop and mobile observation of the visible path.
- Checks for headline, next-action, CTA destination, stated pricing or trial
  language, domain, and public trust-page consistency.
- Up to 10 prioritized findings with the public URL, observation time,
  reproduction steps, evidence, and a bounded recommendation.
- A one-page summary and a 15-minute walkthrough.
- Target delivery within two business days after the public starting URL,
  target visitor action, scope, and delivery date are confirmed in writing.

## Excluded

No login, account creation, field entry, form submission, purchase, security or
penetration test, captcha bypass, private/customer-data review, legal or
accessibility certification, production change, implementation, or outcome
guarantee is included.

## Evidence standard

Every finding is marked as a reproducible observation or a clearly framed
question. A clean route is reported as a pass; the report is not padded with
speculative defects.

## Price and next step

The fixed fee is **$350**. Before work or payment, the buyer and Allure Labs
must confirm the public starting URL, target visitor action, scope, price,
delivery date, acceptance boundary, and communication channel in writing. A
lawful payment arrangement is established only after that written scope is
accepted through an authorized route.
